SPIDERman

In this gritty MCU Phase Six chapter, a completely isolated Peter Parker navigates a bleak new reality in a rundown New York tenement, entirely forgotten by the world after the reality-shattering spell of No Way Home. Stripped of his high-tech Stark suits and elite Avengers connections, the web-slinger is forced to embrace a raw, street-level existence while battling an elusive, mind-hijacking psionic threat targeting the Department of Damage Control. As the crushing emotional toll of losing MJ pushes his psychological limits, Peter undergoes an aggressive physical evolution, turning his solo crusade into an intense fight for survival that tests the very definition of a neighborhood hero.
